# Roadmap

Strata is in beta. You can use the form diagram, point markers, the beat grid, commentary, and sharing today. The beta is for polishing what exists with real analysts. Version 2 adds new widgets and a tool for corpus work. The phases follow readiness rather than a calendar, so there are no dates.

## The plan

| Phase | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Now**, in the beta | Everything in the [README](../README.md): the form diagram, point markers, the beat grid and click track, commentary, sharing, embedding, and export. |
| **During the beta** | A guided walkthrough for your first analysis, a review of the built-in vocabulary with analysts, and a set of example analyses across repertoires. |
| **Version 2** | An **energy contour** widget, where you trace the rise and fall of intensity as levels you choose. Because you choose levels rather than drawing freehand, you can compare shapes across tracks. Version 2 also brings the **Corpus Builder**, a companion tool where you gather many `.strata` files and ask questions across them. It also gives readers of an embed controls to loop a passage and show its context. |
| **Later**, possible but not set | Ideas with a design but no commitment. With an instrumentation widget, you would show which parts play when. With a score link, you would work in the bars of an encoded score (MEI) rather than the seconds of a recording, in version 3 at the earliest. Other people may build widgets too, such as a harmonic rhythm or melodic contour view. |

## Not planned

- **Automatic section detection.** You mark what you hear. Strata doesn't analyze audio. YouTube doesn't give it access to the audio anyway.
- **A public library of analyses** with accounts, ratings, and comments. Strata hosts nothing, so sharing a file stays up to you. This could change if the community asks for it.

## How to influence it

To suggest a feature or a vocabulary pack, click **Report an issue** in the app or open an issue on GitHub. Say what your research or teaching needs, so it can shape what comes next.
